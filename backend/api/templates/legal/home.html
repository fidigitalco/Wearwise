WearWise AI

Your AI fashion shopping assistant

WearWise learns your style, sizes and budget, then recommends real, in-stock products from trusted stores. Every recommendation opens the retailer's own product page, where the purchase happens.

What the app does

My style

Sizes for each kind of item, budget, favourite styles and colours, the stores the user likes and things they never want to see.

Smart search

Describe what you want in plain words. Results are filtered by size, budget, rating and delivery to your country.

For you

Daily picks and outfit ideas built around the user's profile and the weather.

Hunts

"Find me this": the app keeps looking and sends a notification when a matching product appears or drops in price.

Photo match

Upload a photo of a look and get similar products you can actually buy.

Wardrobe & stylist

A digital wardrobe, outfit builder and AI stylist that suggests pieces to complete what the user already owns.

How we work with retailers

  1. We show products from your feed or catalogue that match the user's style, size and budget.
  2. The user taps a product and lands on your product page through a tracked affiliate link.
  3. The purchase, payment and delivery happen on your site.
Ranking is based only on style match, product quality, reviews and price. Commission never affects ranking, and sponsored items are always labelled. No brand bidding, coupon or cashback traffic.
